What is KCET 2027 counselling?

The Karnataka Common Entrance Test (KCET) is conducted by the Karnataka Examinations Authority (KEA) for admission to first-year Engineering, Technology, Architecture, Pharmacy, Naturopathy and several other professional degree programmes across Karnataka. After the KCET 2027 rank is published, eligible candidates register on the KEA counselling portal, verify documents, enter college-and-course options, go through mock allotment, and are allotted seats across multiple rounds based on rank, category and choice order. KCET 2027 counselling will govern admissions for the 2027–28 academic session.

Eligibility

  • Passed 10+2 (or equivalent) with Physics and Mathematics as compulsory subjects, plus Chemistry/Biology/Biotechnology/Electronics/Computer Science as applicable
  • Minimum qualifying marks as prescribed by KEA for the applicable category
  • Valid KCET rank for the relevant course category
  • Karnataka eligibility — by residence, study, or hyderabad-karnataka/kalyana-karnataka provisions — required for reserved-category and state-quota seats

Courses & institutions

  • Government and university engineering colleges in Karnataka
  • Private and deemed-to-be-university engineering colleges participating in KCET 2027 counselling
  • Pharmacy, Architecture, Naturopathy & Yoga, and other listed professional courses
  • Seat matrix and course-wise intake published separately by KEA before each round

Documents required

  • KCET 2027 rank card / scorecard
  • Class 10 and Class 12 marksheets and pass certificates
  • Karnataka study, residence or eligibility certificate, as applicable
  • Category certificate (SC/ST/OBC/Cat-1/2A/2B/3A/3B) and income certificate where relevant
  • Migration certificate, transfer certificate, and Aadhaar or other valid photo ID
  • Passport-size photograph and scanned signature

Fee structure

  • Document verification fee at the time of counselling registration
  • Option-entry / choice-locking fee for each round entered
  • Seat acceptance and confirmation fee after allotment
  • College-specific tuition and other fees payable directly at the allotted institute

Exact amounts are fixed by KEA each year — refer to the official KCET 2027 counselling notification before making any payment.

Participating colleges & reservation categories

College types

  • Government and university engineering colleges
  • Government-aided institutes
  • Private, unaided and deemed university colleges

Reservation categories

  • Karnataka categories — GM, 2A, 2B, 3A, 3B, SC, ST, Category-1
  • Rural, Kannada medium, and Hyderabad-Karnataka (Article 371J) reservation
  • Persons with Disability (PwD) horizontal reservation
  • Defence and ex-servicemen quota seats

KCET 2027 counselling process

  1. Register on the official KEA / KCET 2027 counselling portal using rank card credentials.
  2. Complete document verification — online upload or at a designated centre, as notified.
  3. Pay the counselling fee applicable for the round.
  4. Enter college and course options in order of genuine preference; use mock allotment to preview outcomes and revise options.
  5. Check the seat allotment result and choose to accept, freeze, or move to the next round as per the rules for that round.
  6. Pay the confirmation fee and report to the allotted college within the given window to secure the seat.

How the merit list works

  • KCET rank is derived from the entrance test score combined with Class 12 marks as per KEA's weightage formula
  • Within each category, candidates are ranked and matched to options in order during each round
  • Ties are resolved as per KEA's published tie-breaking criteria
  • A candidate is allotted the highest-preference option their rank makes possible in that round

Option entry — what matters

  • Enter every college-course combination you'd genuinely accept, not just the top few
  • Use the mock allotment result to sanity-check your ranking of options before the real round
  • Options can usually be edited or reordered until the entry window for that round closes
  • An incomplete or unsaved option list can mean no allotment in that round

Scholarships & fee concessions

  • Karnataka government fee reimbursement schemes for eligible SC/ST/OBC/minority candidates
  • Post-matric scholarships for income-eligible categories, applied for after admission
  • National-level scholarship schemes candidates may separately qualify for
  • Some private colleges offer merit-based fee concessions — check directly with the institute

Important instructions

  • Use a working mobile number and email — all KEA communication is sent through these
  • Keep scanned documents ready in the specified format and size before starting registration
  • Document verification and option-entry fees are generally non-refundable
  • Once a seat is accepted and frozen, exit or upgrade options follow KEA's published policy for that round

Helpdesk & support

  • Official KEA helpdesk contact details are published with each counselling notification
  • For portal login or technical issues, use the support contact listed on the KEA/KCET website
  • For document or eligibility queries, help centres designated for document verification can also assist
  • Always cross-check any phone number or email against the official KEA portal before sharing personal documents

Frequently asked questions

Is KCET compulsory for Karnataka engineering admissions?

Yes, for Karnataka state quota seats in engineering, pharmacy and several other professional courses, a valid KCET rank is required. COMEDK and JEE Main also feed separate seat pools at some institutions.

Can candidates from outside Karnataka apply through KCET?

KCET 2027 counselling primarily serves Karnataka-eligible candidates for state quota seats; candidates without Karnataka eligibility generally compete for seats under different quotas such as the All India or management quota.

What is mock allotment in KCET 2027 counselling?

Mock allotment is a trial round based on the options a candidate has entered so far. It shows a likely seat outcome without being final, letting candidates revise their option list before the actual round.

How many rounds of KCET 2027 counselling are held?

KEA typically conducts multiple seat allotment rounds followed by an extended or supplementary round, depending on seats remaining.

What documents are required for KCET 2027 counselling?

KCET rank card, Class 10 and 12 marksheets, Karnataka eligibility/study/residence certificates as applicable, category and income certificates, migration certificate, and a valid photo ID.

What is the KCET 2027 counselling fee?

The fee differs by category and is notified by KEA each year, generally including a document verification fee and a seat acceptance/confirmation fee.

How is the KCET merit list prepared?

Merit is based on the KCET rank, calculated from the entrance test score combined with Class 12 marks as per KEA's weightage formula, within each eligible category.

Are KCET 2027 counselling fees refundable?

Document verification and option-entry related fees are generally non-refundable; seat cancellation refunds, where allowed, follow KEA's published schedule and deductions.
