The 80-Minute Session Breakdown

Each subject examination is allocated an 80-minute window:

  • Initial 10 Minutes (10:30–10:40 AM / 02:30–02:40 PM): Verification of Question Booklet serial number, entering Candidate Name and CET Admission Ticket Number, and shading the exact Question Booklet Version Code on the OMR sheet.
  • Next 70 Minutes (10:40–11:50 AM / 02:40–03:50 PM): Unsealing the question booklet and darkening bubbles for the 60 questions. You have exactly 1.16 minutes per question.

Official Bell Schedule & Gate Timings

KEA adheres to strict synchronized bell timings across all test centers:

  • 09:45 AM / 01:45 PM: Gates Open for security frisking and document check.
  • 10:15 AM / 02:15 PM: Candidates must occupy their allotted desk. (Last entry allowed is 10:25 AM / 02:25 PM).
  • 10:30 AM / 02:30 PM (First Bell): OMR Answer Sheets & Question Booklets distributed.
  • 11:45 AM / 03:45 PM (Warning Bell): 5 minutes remaining alert.
  • 11:50 AM / 03:50 PM (Final Bell): Cease writing; invigilators collect original OMRs.

KCET 2027 Exam Pattern & Course Evaluation

Standardized pen-and-paper optical examination structured around objective Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs) mapped to 1st & 2nd PUC syllabi.

Marking Rules

• +1 Mark: For every correctly shaded single circle on the OMR.
• 0 Marks: For unattempted questions or wrongly shaded bubbles.
• Zero Negative Marking: No deductions whatsoever. Attempt every single question!
• Multiple Bubbles: If more than one bubble is shaded for a question, it is deemed invalid (0 marks).

Medium & Question Booklet

• Bilingual Format: All questions appear in both English and Kannada parallelly.
• Discrepancy Rule: In case of any translation ambiguity, the English version is legally final.
• 16 Version Codes: Question booklets are printed in 16 randomized permutations (e.g., A1–A4, B1–B4, C1–C4, D1–D4) to eliminate copying.

OMR Paper Architecture

• Two-Tier Replica Sheet: The OMR comprises Top Original Sheet and Bottom Replica Carbon/Impression sheet.
• Ballpoint Pen Only: Shading must be executed strictly with blue or black ballpoint pen.
• Candidate Retains Replica: You take home the replica sheet to calculate marks against official answer keys!

The 50:50 Normalization Mechanism Explained

Unlike JEE Main which ranks candidates solely on entrance performance, KCET uses an egalitarian composite ranking system giving equal weight to board examinations and entrance scores.

The Mathematical Formula

Composite Score (out of 100) =
[ (KCET PCM Marks / 180) × 50 ]
+ [ (12th Board PCM Marks / 300) × 50 ]

Step 1: Calculate your KCET PCM total out of 180 (Physics 60 + Chemistry 60 + Math 60). Multiply by (50 / 180) to obtain your KCET share out of 50.

Step 2: Calculate your 12th Board PCM aggregate out of 300 (Physics 100 + Chemistry 100 + Math 100). Multiply by (50 / 300) to obtain your Board share out of 50.

Step 3: Sum both scores to obtain the Composite Merit Score out of 100.

Tie-Breaking Hierarchy Rules

When two or more candidates obtain identical composite merit scores out of 100, KEA applies the statutory tie-breaking cascade in strict serial order:

  1. Rule 1: Higher marks obtained in KCET Mathematics.
  2. Rule 2: Higher marks obtained in KCET Physics.
  3. Rule 3: Higher marks obtained in KCET Chemistry.
  4. Rule 4: Higher marks obtained in 12th Board Mathematics.
  5. Rule 5: Higher marks obtained in 12th Board Physics.
  6. Rule 6: Higher marks obtained in 12th Board Chemistry.
  7. Rule 7: If tie still persists, candidate older in age is assigned the higher merit rank.

OMR Sheet Instructions & Scanner Safety Rules

KCET uses high-speed Optical Mark Reader (OMR) scanners. A minor clerical mistake on the OMR header can cause entire papers to be rejected without human review.

Fatal OMR Mistakes (Avoid at All Costs)

  • Incorrect Version Code: The optical scanner evaluates your answers against the answer key corresponding to the Version Code shaded on the OMR. If you omit or shade the wrong version code, the paper receives 0 marks.
  • Using Gel or Ink Pens: Wet ink smudges under the scanner rollers, destroying multiple rows. Use strictly firm blue or black ballpoint pens.
  • Whitener, Eraser or Blades: Applying correction fluid, erasing, or scratching with a blade is treated as malpractice and voids the sheet.
  • Multiple Shading: If you darken two circles for the same question, the scanner automatically awards 0 marks.

Correct Shading Best Practices

  • Complete Darkening: Darken the circle completely so the letter inside (A, B, C, or D) is fully covered. Faint ticks, crosses, or half-circles are ignored by the optical scanner.
  • Pre-Bell Verification: Use the 10-minute window before the start bell to verify that your Roll Number, Admission Ticket Number, and Question Booklet Serial Number are written and shaded accurately.
  • Check Question Serial Number: Regularly cross-check that question #27 in the booklet matches circle #27 on the OMR to avoid the dreaded "offset shading error".
  • Retain Green Replica: Ensure the invigilator separates and hands you the bottom replica sheet before leaving the hall.
Question Booklet Version Code Matching Rule

Your question booklet cover page prominently features a bold letter-number code (such as A-2 or C-4). The invigilator will sign your OMR sheet only after verifying that the version code written and darkened on your OMR matches your booklet. Do NOT leave the hall without the invigilator's physical signature on your OMR.

KCET 2027 Hall Ticket & Test Day Protocol

The KEA Admission Ticket (Admit Card) is published online approximately 10 to 14 days before the exam. Know what to carry and what is strictly prohibited.

Download Procedure

1. Visit KEA official portal cetonline.karnataka.gov.in/kea.
2. Click on UGCET 2027 Admission Ticket.
3. Enter your Application Number and Date of Birth (DD-MM-YYYY).
4. Download and print at least 3 color copies on clean A4 paper.

Mandatory Items to Carry

• Printed KCET Hall Ticket: With clear candidate photograph and signature.
• Original Photo ID: Aadhaar Card, Passport, PAN Card, College ID, or Driving License.
• Pens: 2 to 3 good quality Blue or Black ballpoint pens.
• Water Bottle: 500ml transparent bottle (no labels).

Strictly Barred Items

• Electronic Devices: Mobile phones, smartwatches, digital wristwatches, bluetooth devices.
• Study Materials: Log tables, slide rules, calculators, notebooks, loose blank papers.
• Accessories: Metallic jewelry, heavy belts, caps, or shaded sunglasses.

Horanadu & Gadinadu Kannada Language Examination

Mandatory language proficiency test conducted for candidates whose mother tongue is Kannada, Tulu, or Kodava but who completed their 10+2 outside Karnataka State.

Test Structure & Syllabus

• Total Marks: 50 Marks (Single paper of 1-hour duration).
• Minimum Qualifying Threshold: 12 Marks (24%) out of 50.
• Syllabus Scope: Karnataka State Board 4th Standard primary school Kannada reader syllabus.
• Question Types: Basic grammar, vocabulary, sentence completion, reading comprehension passage, and short translation.

Test Centres & Exemption Criteria

• Designated Test Centres: Bengaluru, Belagavi, Bidar, Ballari, and Mangaluru.
• Exemption: Candidates who have studied Kannada as first, second, or third language for a minimum of 10 full academic years (from 1st to 10th std) are exempt upon submitting certified study certificates.
• Crucial Warning: Scoring less than 12 marks disqualifies the candidate from the entire Karnataka state quota counselling!

For Engineering & Technology ranks, KEA computes a composite score out of 100 by giving 50% weightage to the candidate's KCET entrance marks in PCM (Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics out of 180 converted to 50) and 50% weightage to Class 12th / 2nd PUC Board examination marks in PCM (Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics out of 300 converted to 50). Each KCET subject paper (Biology, Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry) is conducted in an 80-minute time block. The remaining 70 minutes are allotted for answering the 60 multiple-choice questions (1 mark each).

If the version code bubble is omitted, shaded incorrectly, or shaded with multiple bubbles, the scanner cannot evaluate the OMR sheet, resulting in zero marks for that paper.
