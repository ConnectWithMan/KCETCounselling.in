How the KCET College Predictor 2027 Works

Built upon historical Karnataka Examinations Authority (KEA) seat matrices, horizontal sub-quota relaxation formulas, and real JoSAA/NEET seat surrender vacant shifts for KCET 2027 admissions.

Phase 1: Normalization

50:50 Composite Normalization

Unlike JEE Main (entrance only) or state board admissions (board only), KCET merit ranks are derived giving equal 50% weightage to KCET PCM marks (out of 180) and 50% weightage to 2nd PUC / Class 12 board marks (out of 300). Our predictor models this composite normalization to estimate realistic merit thresholds.

Phase 2: Category Quotas

Vertical & Horizontal Quotas

The engine applies Karnataka's statutory reservation percentages (GM 44%, 2A 15%, 2B 4%, 3A 4%, 3B 5%, SC 15%, ST 3%). It further layers horizontal sub-quotas like 15% Rural (Form 1/2), 5% Kannada Medium, and Article 371(J) Hyderabad-Karnataka which relax cutoffs by 15% to 50%.

6 Critical Option Entry Pitfalls to Avoid

Every counselling season, thousands of deserving candidates forfeit seats due to minor procedural mistakes. Protect your engineering allotment with these battle-tested rules.

1. The "15 Options" Mistake

Entering only 10 to 15 colleges is the primary reason behind "Zero Seat Allotted" in Round 1. KEA charges zero penalty and places no cap on entries. Enter at least 60 to 100+ choices spanning dream, target, and realistic safety backups.

2. Reversing Priority Order

The KEA matching algorithm evaluates choices strictly sequentially (#1, #2, #3...). If you put a low-tier backup college as Option #4 and your dream college as Option #10, the moment Option #4 qualifies, all choices below it are permanently discarded.

3. Skipping Separate SNQ Option Codes

If eligible for the 5% Supernumerary Quota (family annual income < ₹8.00 Lakhs), you must enter the college's SNQ course code separately above the regular seat code. SNQ offers a 100% tuition fee waiver, reducing annual fees to nearly zero.

Check SNQ Fee Waiver Rules

4. Choice 1 Mandatory Reporting Rule

Selecting Choice 1 ("Satisfied & Freeze") legally binds you to pay fees, download the admission order, and physically report to the institution before the KEA deadline. Failing to report results in seat cancellation and expulsion from subsequent rounds.

Choice 1 Reporting Protocols & Deadlines

5. Treating Mock Allotment as Final

The Mock Allotment result is only an educational preview. Over 65,000+ candidates modify, re-order, and insert new choices during the 48-hour post-mock edit window, causing actual Round 1 cutoffs to shift by 10% to 25%. Always review and refine after mock results.

6. Hasty Deletion of Higher Options in Round 2

When opting for Choice 2 in Round 1, candidates are free to delete or re-sequence options above their held seat. However, deleting aspirational top-5 colleges because of tight mock cutoffs prevents you from catching sudden vacant surrender seats in Round 2.

KEA Seat Allotment: Choice 1, 2, 3 & 4 Decision Architecture

Once KEA releases your seat allotment result, you must exercise one of four statutory choices within the declared timeframe. Selecting the wrong option can forfeit your admission.

Choice 1

Satisfied & Accept

Seat Locked • Exits Counselling

Candidate is 100% satisfied with the allotted seat and does not wish to participate in subsequent rounds for higher options.

  • Pay prescribed tuition fee to KEA
  • Download official Admission Order
  • Report physically to college with original verification docs
Choice 2

Hold & Seek Higher

Holds Seat • Enters Round 2

Candidate is satisfied with the current seat but wishes to be considered for higher priority choices in Round 2.

  • Pays fee to secure current seat as backup
  • If higher option is allotted in Round 2, old seat is automatically released
  • If no higher option is allotted, original seat remains 100% safe
Choice 3

Surrender & Upgrade

Releases Seat • Enters Round 2

Candidate rejects the allotted seat completely and enters Round 2 to compete for higher options.

  • No fee payment required in Round 1
  • Surrendered seat is permanently re-allocated to others
  • High risk if higher choices fail to allot in Round 2
Choice 4

Quit Counselling

Exits KEA • Zero Claims

Candidate is dissatisfied with the allotment and opts out of the entire KEA counselling process.

  • Allotted seat is released back to pool
  • Barred from Round 2 and Extended rounds
  • Candidate is free to join IIT/NIT/JoSAA or other quotas
