Document Verification & 16-Character Secret Key

The physical or electronic verification of eligibility documents is the gateway to KCET Counselling. Understand how the KEA Verification Slip is generated and how to safeguard your Secret Key.

Confidential Security Notice: Your 16-character alphanumeric Secret Key is strictly personal. It grants full administrative control over your college option list, choice locking, and seat surrender. Never reveal your secret key or password to cyber cafes, education consultants, or unauthorized portals.

Track 1: Online Auto-Verification

Applicable to candidates who completed 2nd PUC from Karnataka State PU Board, whose 7-year Karnataka study records match the Department of School Education SATS database, and whose caste/income certificates are authenticated via 15-digit Revenue Department (RD) numbers.

  • No physical visit to BEO office required
  • Verification Slip downloaded directly from KEA website
  • Instant receipt of 16-character Secret Key online

Track 2: Physical BEO Verification

Mandatory for CBSE, CISCE/ICSE, J&K migrants, Horanadu/Gadinadu Kannadigas, and candidates whose SATS or RD verification showed mismatch. You must visit the designated Block Education Officer (BEO) with original study and caste certificates.

  • 7-Year Study Certificate signed by Head of Institution & BEO/DDPI
  • Rural (1st–10th) & Kannada Medium (1st–10th) BEO endorsements
  • Verification officer issues stamped physical slip with Secret Key

Option Entry Step-by-Step Blueprint

Option Entry is the single most critical phase of KCET Counselling. The seat allocation algorithm allocates seats based on your priority numbers. Master the three-tier basket framework.

Step 1: First Login

Candidate Activation

Navigate to KEA Option Entry portal. Enter your CET Number, 16-Character Secret Key, and create a strong customized password. Set a security hint question and register your OTP-verified mobile number.

Step 2: Preference Entry

Course-College Selection

Filter institutions by District, Management Type (Govt, Aided, Private), and Discipline (Engineering, CS, AI, EC). Assign priority numbers (1, 2, 3, 4...) sequentially without skipping numbers or assigning duplicate ranks.

Step 3: Verification & Print

Locking & Draft Copy

Regularly click Save & Update. The portal auto-locks choices upon deadline expiry. Always download and print the Detailed Option Entry Work Sheet for audit verification.

The Three-Tier Option Entry Basket Strategy

Never limit your choices to just 10 or 15 colleges. KEA allows unlimited options. Top counsellors recommend structuring your preference sheet into three distinct probability tiers:

Tier 1: Dream Choices (30%)

Aspirational / High-Cutoff Seats

Premium colleges and top branches (RVCE CSE, BMSCE ISE, MSRIT AIML) whose previous year cutoffs are higher than your rank. If vacancies open up due to student dropouts, you get the benefit.

Tier 2: Realistic Choices (40%)

Rank-Matched Target Colleges

Established colleges where your KCET composite rank falls comfortably between the previous year's Round 1 opening and closing cutoffs. These form the core anchor of your admission probability.

Tier 3: Safe Backups (30%)

Guaranteed Safety Anchors

Quality accredited institutions where historical closing ranks are significantly below your rank. Entering these ensures you are never left with an empty allotment across Round 1 and Round 2.

Mock Allotment Mechanics & Calibration

Mock Allotment is a trial simulation run by KEA before final seat allocation. Learn how to interpret mock results and optimize your choice priorities before the final locking deadline.

What is Mock Allotment?

Mock Allotment tests your submitted options against the actual options of all other registered KCET candidates. It gives you a clear, objective snapshot of which college and branch you would receive if options were frozen at that moment.

  • Not a Guaranteed Seat: Mock allotment is strictly indicative, not binding.
  • Cutoff Trend Analysis: KEA publishes category-wise mock cutoff ranks for all colleges.
  • Dynamic Shifts: Final allotment cutoffs typically shift by 5% to 15% as candidates modify choices post-mock.

Actionable Steps After Mock Results

KEA opens the portal for 48 to 72 hours immediately following Mock Allotment. You must execute one of three actions:

Got Desired Seat: Retain your preferences or add even higher dream institutions above it. Never place an inferior option above your mock allotted college.
Got Lower Preference Seat: Review options between your top choice and allotted seat. Delete unwanted lower branches so you don't get trapped in Round 1.
No Seat Allotted: Your options were overly ambitious. Immediately add 30 to 50 realistic and safe colleges with higher closing cutoffs to prevent getting zero seats.

Post-Allotment Decision Architecture: Choice 1, 2, 3 & 4

When Round 1 seat allocation is declared, every candidate allotted a seat MUST exercise one of four statutory choices within the specified KEA deadline. Failing to choose forfeits your seat entirely.

Choice 1: Freeze

Accept & Join

Candidate is 100% satisfied with the allotted seat and does not wish to participate in any subsequent rounds.

  • Must pay full tuition fee online
  • Download KEA Admission Order
  • Report to college before deadline
  • Exits all subsequent KEA rounds
Choice 2: Float

Hold & Upgrade

Candidate is satisfied with allotted seat, but wishes to compete in Round 2 for HIGHER preference options only.

  • Must pay tuition fee to hold seat
  • Held seat is 100% safe & reserved
  • If upgraded in R2, R1 seat is cancelled
  • If not upgraded, R1 seat remains yours
Choice 3: Slide

Reject & Re-enter

Candidate is dissatisfied with allotted seat, surrenders it back to the vacancy pool, but enters Round 2.

  • Allotted seat is permanently forfeited
  • No tuition fee payment in Round 1
  • Eligible for Round 2 higher options
  • Risk of getting no seat in R2
Choice 4: Exit

Quit Counselling

Candidate is completely dissatisfied with the allotted seat and wishes to quit the entire KEA admission process.

  • Surrenders seat back to KEA pool
  • Zero fee payment required
  • Disqualified from all further rounds
  • Free to join COMEDK / Management

Supernumerary Quota (SNQ) 100% Fee Waiver

AICTE-mandated 5% extra seats created over and above sanctioned college intake, offering zero tuition fee for academically meritorious students from low-income families:

Income Eligibility: Total annual family income from all sources must be strictly less than ₹8.00 Lakhs as validated by an online 15-digit Revenue Department RD number.
Automatic Algorithm Evaluation: Candidates do NOT need to fill separate option codes. The KEA system automatically evaluates you for SNQ seats across all colleges in your choice list.
Zero Tuition Fee: Allotted students pay ₹0 tuition fee. You only remit nominal university development and examination fees (~₹4,100 to ₹8,500/year depending on university).
Merit Criteria: Awarded purely on KCET rank merit among eligible applicants within that specific institution and branch.

Round 2 & 2nd Extended Round (Mop-Up / Casual Vacancy)

How subsequent rounds operate, what constitutes consequential vacancies, and why 2nd Extended Round participation carries strict seat forfeiture and legal penalty conditions.

Round 2 Dynamics

Seat pool comprises unallotted seats from Round 1, surrendered seats from Choice 3, cancelled seats from Choice 4, and newly sanctioned government intakes. Candidates with Choice 2 & Choice 3 participate.

Consequential Vacancies

When a Choice 2 candidate upgrades to a higher preference seat in Round 2, their previously held seat is released instantaneously. The software immediately allocates this vacated seat to the next eligible rank.

2nd Extended (Casual Vacancy)

Final mop-up round to eliminate vacant seats. Reserved seats with no remaining applicants are converted to General Merit (GM) as per the statutory de-reservation matrix.

Strict 2nd Extended Round Rule: Any seat allotted in the 2nd Extended Round is final and mandatory. There are no Choice 2, 3, or 4 options. Candidates must accept the seat, remit fees, and report to the college. Surrendering an allotted seat post-extended round results in complete fee forfeiture and statutory legal penalties.
