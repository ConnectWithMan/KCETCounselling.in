Our Mission

Every year, over 2.5 lakh candidates in Karnataka appear for the UGCET (KCET). While students work tirelessly to score well in Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics, many face substantial confusion when the actual counselling and seat allocation process begins.

Our mission is to eliminate information asymmetry. We provide clear, objective, and data-backed tools that translate complex government gazettes and KEA notification brochures into actionable steps for every parent and student across Karnataka.

  • Demystify the 50:50 composite score ranking mechanism.
  • Guide students through BEO verification and 15-digit RD numbers.
  • Protect candidates from fraudulent agents and paid seat rackets.

Our Vision

We envision an admissions process where every Karnataka student—regardless of whether they studied in a metropolitan private school in Bengaluru or a rural government school in Raichur—has equal access to authoritative counselling insights.

By simplifying the option entry choice architecture and highlighting special reservation quotas (Rural 15%, Kannada Medium 5%, Kalyana Karnataka 371J, and Supernumerary SNQ Quota), we ensure students maximize their rightful merit.

  • Complete transparency in Karnataka engineering seat matrices.
  • Dedicated coverage for rural and vernacular medium candidates.
  • Free access to mock allotment strategy simulations.
