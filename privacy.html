1. Introduction & Scope

Welcome to KCET Counselling 2027 (accessible at https://kcetcounselling.in/). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal data of students, parents, and educators who use our advisory guides and cutoff tools.

This Privacy Policy explains how our portal collects, uses, shares, and safeguards information when you visit our website. Our practices comply with the Information Technology Act, 2000, the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011, and the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023 of India.

Independent Entity Clarification: kcetcounselling.in is an independent educational informational portal. We are not affiliated with the Karnataka Examinations Authority (KEA). Official government notifications are hosted separately at cetonline.karnataka.gov.in/kea.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information in two primary ways: information you provide voluntarily and automated log data.

A. Information You Voluntarily Provide

  • Inquiries & Feedback: When you reach out to our advisory team via email or our Contact Helpdesk, we retain your email address and message details solely to answer your questions and provide guidance.
  • Tool Inputs (Client-Side): Marks, ranks, or category filters entered into our College Predictor or Normalization Calculator are processed locally in your web browser memory. They are not stored in our databases or linked to your personal identity.

B. Automated Technical Information (Log Files)

Like most standard websites, kcetcounselling.in automatically logs non-personally identifiable technical information when you browse our pages. This includes:

  • Internet Protocol (IP) address (anonymized)
  • Browser type and version (e.g. Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge)
  • Operating system and device category (Mobile, Tablet, Desktop)
  • Referring URL and exit pages
  • Date and time stamps of visits and pages viewed

3. Cookies, Web Beacons & Google AdSense

Our website uses cookies (small text files stored on your device) to maintain user preferences, ensure fast page loading, and analyze traffic trends.

Google AdSense & DoubleClick DART Cookies

We use Google AdSense to serve advertisements that support the free operation of our educational platform.

  • Google is a third-party vendor that uses cookies to serve ads on our site.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • Alternatively, you can opt out of third-party vendor use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting aboutads.info.

4. Web Analytics (Google Analytics 4)

We use Google Analytics to understand how students navigate our cutoff charts, documentation checklists, and option entry guides.

Google Analytics collects aggregated, anonymized information about visitor behavior. IP anonymization is enabled by default. We do not transmit personally identifiable information (such as your name, Aadhaar number, or KEA CET application number) to Google Analytics.

5. Web Push Notifications (OneSignal)

With your explicit opt-in consent, we utilize OneSignal SDK to send browser notifications regarding urgent KEA deadlines, mock allotment publications, and seat allocation rounds.

These notifications do not access your personal contacts. You can withdraw consent or turn off notifications at any time directly through your web browser's notification preferences.

6. How We Use Your Information

We use the data we collect solely for educational, technical, and operational purposes:

  • To provide, maintain, and enhance the functionality of our website.
  • To respond to your inquiries, cutoff questions, and feedback.
  • To monitor traffic patterns and resolve server errors.
  • To protect our infrastructure against cyber attacks, denial-of-service attempts, and automated web scrapers.

Zero Commercial Sale Guarantee: We never sell, lease, rent, or trade your personal contact information to third-party telemarketers, coaching institutes, private engineering colleges, or commercial brokers.

7. Data Storage & Security Safeguards

We employ standard industry security protocols to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure:

  • All data transmissions between your browser and our servers are encrypted using modern Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS).
  • Access to contact submissions is restricted strictly to authorized advisory personnel.
  • We do not store sensitive payment details, NetBanking credentials, or KEA Secret Keys.

8. Children's Online Privacy Protection

Protecting the privacy of minors is especially vital. Our educational content is intended for high school graduates, pre-university students (PUC / Class 12), and their guardians. We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13.

If you believe that a child under 13 has provided personal data on our portal, please contact us immediately, and we will promptly delete such records from our systems.

9. Your Rights Under the DPDP Act 2023

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, you have clear rights regarding your personal information:

  • Right to Access: You may request a summary of the personal data you have submitted to us.
  • Right to Rectification & Erasure: You may request the correction of inaccurate details or the complete deletion of your submitted contact records.
  • Right of Grievance Redressal: You have the right to register a grievance with our Data Grievance Officer regarding data handling.
