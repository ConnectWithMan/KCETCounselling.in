Foundational Pillars of KCET 2027 Eligibility

To claim government quota engineering, architecture, farm science, pharmacy, or nursing seats through KEA, every candidate must satisfy two independent legal tests: Nativity/Domicile criteria (Clauses A–O) and Academic Qualification benchmarks.

1. Domicile & Nativity

Candidate must fulfill at least one of the 16 statutory clauses (A to O). Government quota seats in Karnataka are legally restricted to candidates having deep educational or native lineage in Karnataka.

2. Academic Qualifications

Candidate must have passed 2nd PUC / 12th Standard or equivalent with compulsory subjects (Physics & Mathematics for Engineering; PCB for Agriculture/Pharmacy/Nursing) with minimum prescribed aggregate scores.

3. BEO Counter-Signature

Every study certificate must be signed by the school headmaster and officially counter-signed with seal by the jurisdictional Block Education Officer (BEO) or DDPI for CBSE/ICSE schools.

4. SATS & RD Verification

Candidates who studied under Karnataka State Syllabus must furnish their 9-digit Student Achievement Tracking System (SATS) number for direct electronic verification against Department of School Education records.

Non-Karnataka Students Notice: Government Quota Ineligibility

Candidates who do not satisfy any of the Clauses from A to O are classified as Non-Karnataka Candidates. Non-Karnataka candidates cannot claim government quota seats in Engineering, Architecture, or Farm Science courses. They may only participate for institutional management quota seats or take COMEDK UGET for private engineering seats in Karnataka.

Strict No-Rounding-Off Rule for Academic Percentage

KEA strictly enforces the no-rounding-off rule. A General Merit candidate with 44.99% aggregate in PCM is legally ineligible for engineering admission (mandatory threshold is 45.00%). Similarly, a reserved category candidate with 39.99% in PCM is legally ineligible (mandatory threshold is 40.00%).

Most Common (85%+ Candidates) Clause A

Clause A: 7-Year Karnataka Education

Requires cumulative schooling of at least 7 academic years in Karnataka from 1st to 12th standard, and passing either 10th (SSLC) or 12th (PUC) from Karnataka. Years need not be continuous.

Required Documents

• 7-Year Study Certificate signed by Headmaster and BEO/DDPI
• 10th Marks Card & 12th Marks Card
• SATS 9-digit number for State Board schools

Parent Lineage Quota Clause B

Clause B: Parent 7-Year Education

Applies to candidates who completed both 11th and 12th in Karnataka, and either father or mother studied for 7 full academic years in Karnataka institutions.

Required Documents

• Candidate's 2-Year PUC Study Certificate with BEO seal
• Parent's 7-Year Karnataka Study Certificate / Domicile Certificate
• Parent's 10th/12th certificate or Transfer Certificate (TC)

Linguistic Domicile Clause C & D

Clause C & D: Horanadu / Gadinadu

For Kannada, Tulu, or Kodava mother tongue candidates living outside Karnataka or in border taluks. Mandatory 50-mark Kannada Language Test with 12 marks cutoff.

Required Documents

• Mother tongue affidavit on ₹20 e-stamp paper
• Parent 7-year Karnataka domicile certificate (Clause C)
• Border taluk residence certificate (Clause D)

The 7-Year Karnataka Education Rule (Clause A Deep Dive)

Clause A is the foundation of Karnataka domicile eligibility. Understand how years are counted, how non-continuous schooling works, and how SATS automation simplifies verification.

1

Cumulative 7 Academic Years

Candidate must study for at least 7 full academic years between 1st standard and 12th standard (2nd PUC) in government-run or government-recognized educational institutions situated within the geographical borders of Karnataka.

2

Non-Continuous Schooling Permitted

The 7 years do NOT need to be continuous. For instance, studying 1st to 3rd in Karnataka (3 yrs), 4th to 6th outside (3 yrs), and 7th to 10th in Karnataka (4 yrs) totals 7 years and satisfies Clause A completely.

3

Pass 10th or 12th in Karnataka

In addition to the 7-year study period, the candidate must have appeared for and passed either SSLC (10th Standard) or 2nd PUC (12th Standard) from an institution located in Karnataka.

4

SATS Direct API Verification

Candidates from Karnataka State Board enter their 9-digit SATS number. KEA validates schooling history electronically, eliminating physical verification queues for State Board students.

State Board vs CBSE vs ICSE School Verification

For Karnataka State Syllabus Schools: Study certificates are generated through the school headmaster and verified online via SATS database. If manual verification is required, the certificate must be signed by the school headmaster and counter-signed by the Block Education Officer (BEO).

For CBSE and ICSE Schools in Karnataka: Even though CBSE and ICSE are central boards, if the school is physically located in Karnataka, the candidate's study certificate MUST be counter-signed by the Deputy Director of Public Instruction (DDPI) or jurisdictional BEO. Without this counter-signature and official seal, KEA will reject the document during verification.

Horanadu & Gadinadu Kannadiga Guidelines

Special provisions for Kannada-speaking candidates living outside Karnataka or in border territories. Review syllabus, exam guidelines, and the mandatory 12-mark qualification threshold.

The KEA Kannada Language Examination

To ensure that candidates claiming linguistic domicile possess functional literacy in the state language, KEA conducts a centralized Kannada Language Examination strictly in Bengaluru.

  • Total Marks: 50 Marks (Written test evaluating reading, writing, and comprehension).
  • Minimum Qualifying Cutoff: 12 Marks (24%). Candidates scoring less than 12 marks are declared ineligible.
  • Test Standard: 4th Standard Government Kannada Textbooks level. Includes vocabulary, grammar, sentence formation, and comprehension.
  • Exam Center: Conducted exclusively at the KEA Central Examination Center in Bengaluru.
Statutory Exemptions

Who is Exempt from the Kannada Language Test?

Candidates who satisfy any of the following conditions do NOT need to appear for the KEA Kannada Language Examination:

  • Candidates qualifying under Clause A, B, E, F, G, H, I, J, K, L, M, or N.
  • Candidates qualifying under Clause O (completed 10 years study in Kannada medium outside Karnataka).
  • Candidates who studied 1st to 10th Standard in Kannada Medium in Karnataka.
  • Candidates who passed SSLC (10th) in Karnataka with Kannada as First or Second Language.

Frequently Asked Questions — KCET 2027 Eligibility

Authoritative legal and operational answers to the most common candidate queries regarding KEA clauses, academic marks, BEO signatures, and non-Karnataka status.

To claim government seats in government, private aided, and private unaided engineering colleges in Karnataka, candidates must satisfy at least one of KEA's 16 statutory eligibility clauses (Clauses A to O).

The primary route (Clause A) requires the candidate to have studied for a minimum of 7 full academic years in educational institutions situated in Karnataka from 1st standard to 12th standard/2nd PUC and passed either 10th (SSLC) or 12th (PUC) from Karnataka.

Non-Karnataka students can appear for the KCET entrance examination for specific courses such as B.Sc Nursing, B.Pharm, and Pharma.D in private institutions or under specific institutional management quotas.

However, non-Karnataka candidates CANNOT claim government quota seats in engineering, architecture, or farm science courses under KCET; those seats are strictly reserved for candidates fulfilling Clauses A through O. Non-Karnataka engineering aspirants should apply through COMEDK UGET or institutional management quotas.

Candidates must have passed 2nd PUC / 12th Standard or equivalent with Physics and Mathematics as compulsory subjects, along with Chemistry, Biotechnology, Biology, Computer Science, or Electronics, and with English as one of the languages of study.

General Merit candidates must score a minimum of 45% aggregate marks in the optional subjects (Physics + Mathematics + 3rd subject), while Karnataka SC, ST, Category-1, 2A, 2B, 3A, and 3B candidates require a minimum of 40% aggregate marks. Rounding off (e.g. 44.9% to 45%) is strictly prohibited.

As long as the cumulative period equals or exceeds 7 full academic years and the candidate passes 10th or 12th in Karnataka, they are fully eligible under Clause A.

Clause B applies when the candidate has studied and passed both 1st and 2nd PUC (or 11th and 12th standard) within Karnataka, AND either of the candidate's parents (mother or father) has studied for a minimum of 7 full academic years in educational institutions situated in Karnataka.

In this case, the candidate's 2-year study certificate plus the parent's 7-year Karnataka study certificate or domicile certificate must be produced.
