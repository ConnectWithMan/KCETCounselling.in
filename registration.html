Eligibility Criteria for KCET 2027 Registration

Academic qualifications, minimum percentage thresholds, and domicile prerequisites for Engineering, Architecture, Pharmacy, and Farm Science admissions.

B.E. / B.Tech Engineering

Undergraduate Engineering & Technology

  • Passed 2nd PUC / 12th standard with English as one language.
  • Compulsory subjects: Physics & Mathematics.
  • Optional: Chemistry / Bio-Tech / Biology / Computer Science / Electronics.
  • Min. Marks: 45% aggregate in PCM (40% for SC/ST/OBC Karnataka).

B.Arch (Architecture)

5-Year Architecture Degree

  • Passed 10+2 scheme with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics.
  • Minimum 50% aggregate marks in 10+2 level (45% for reserved categories).
  • NATA Qualification: Must qualify NATA 2027 conducted by CoA.
  • No KCET entrance paper; merit based on 50% NATA + 50% 12th PCM.

B.Sc (Hons) Agriculture & Farm Sciences

Agricultural, Horticultural & Veterinary Courses

  • Passed 2nd PUC / 12th with Physics, Chemistry, Biology, and Mathematics.
  • Agriculturist Quota: 50% seats reserved for children of bona fide agriculturists.
  • Practical Agricultural Test of 200 marks conducted at state Agri varsities.
  • Minimum 50% aggregate for General Merit (40% for SC/ST/Cat-1).

B.Pharma & Pharma.D

Pharmacy & Clinical Sciences

  • Passed 2nd PUC / 12th standard with Physics, Chemistry, Biology/Math.
  • Passed D.Pharm from a PCI recognized institution (for Lateral Entry).
  • Min. Marks: 45% in PCM/PCB (40% for SC/ST/OBC Karnataka).
  • Candidate must have completed 17 years of age as of Dec 31, 2027.

Step-by-Step KCET 2027 Registration Guide

Detailed 7-stage walkthrough of the KEA UGCET online registration process from initial registration to downloading your final application confirmation page.

01

Portal Access & User Account Generation

Initial Setup

Access the official KEA website (cetonline.karnataka.gov.in/kea) and select "UGCET 2027 Online Application". Click on "New User / Registration". Select your citizenship, enter your active personal mobile number and email ID, and create a strong security password.

System Output: A unique 10-digit KCET Application Number is generated and dispatched via SMS. Retain this number for all subsequent logins, admit card downloads, and option entry.
02

SATS Number Validation (Karnataka Board)

Data Integration

For candidates currently enrolled in Karnataka State 2nd PUC, enter your 9-digit Student Achievement Tracking System (SATS) Number. The KEA system cross-references the Department of Pre-University Education database to automatically populate candidate name, father's name, mother's name, date of birth, and college affiliation.

CBSE / ICSE / Other Boards: Candidates from non-state boards select "Other Board" and manually enter their 10th / 12th Roll Number, Board Name, and institutional address.
03

Personal, Domicile & Communication Details

Profile Entry

Enter your Aadhaar number, permanent residential address, communication address, gender, native district, mother tongue, and domicile status. Ensure that your name matches character-by-character with your SSLC / Class 10 marksheet to avoid verification rejection.

Important Check: Ensure the PIN code and district match your official proof of residence.
04

Reservation Quota & 15-Digit RD Number Input

Authentication

Declare your reservation category (General Merit, 2A, 2B, 3A, 3B, SC, ST, Cat-1). If claiming backward class or SC/ST reservation, you must input the 15-digit RD Number of your Caste Certificate issued by Nadakacheri. The KEA portal verifies the certificate's validity online in real time.

Special Quotas: Declare eligibility for Kalyana-Karnataka 371(J) region (Form A), Rural Study (1st to 10th std in non-urban areas), Kannada Medium (1st to 10th std in Kannada), and 5% Supernumerary Quota (SNQ, requiring annual income < ₹8.00 Lakhs).
05

Academic History & 7-Year Study Details

Merit & Domicile

Fill in school-by-school study history from 1st standard through 12th standard / 2nd PUC. For each academic tier, specify the academic year, school name, district, taluk, syllabus (State/CBSE/ICSE), and whether the institution is rural or urban.

Domicile Requirement: To satisfy Clause A, you must establish a continuous or cumulative period of at least seven academic years in Karnataka educational institutions.
06

Scanned Images & Document Upload

Media Specs

Upload scanned JPG/JPEG files according to strict KEA technical dimensions: candidate passport-sized photograph (30 KB to 50 KB), candidate signature (10 KB to 40 KB), left-hand thumb impression (10 KB to 40 KB), and parent signature (10 KB to 40 KB).

Upload Rule: Photographs must be recent, front-facing, against a white or light background without caps or tinted glasses. Selfies or blurred images lead to immediate disqualification.
07

Fee Payment, Declaration & Final Printout

Finalization

Pay the application fee via the integrated payment gateway (UPI, Net Banking, Credit/Debit card) or download the SBI e-challan to pay cash at any branch. Accept the legal declaration, submit the application form, and download your Final Application Confirmation Page.

Mandatory Retention: Take at least 3 printouts of the confirmation page and fee receipt. You will be required to submit this during BEO Document Verification.

SATS & 15-Digit RD Number Verification Guide

How KEA automates candidate credential verification via Karnataka Government state databases and how to troubleshoot common data mismatches.

SATS Number Integration

Department of School Education & Literacy

  • SATS (Student Achievement Tracking System) assigns a unique 9-digit ID to every Karnataka school student.
  • What it Auto-Populates: Full name, father's/mother's name, DOB, school history, and 2nd PUC registration.
  • SATS Not Found? Request your PU College principal to update your enrolment status on the state SATS portal.

15-Digit Nadakacheri RD Number

Karnataka Revenue Department Portal

  • All digitized Caste, Income, and Hyderabad-Karnataka certificates begin with the prefix RD...
  • Real-Time API Check: KEA queries the Nadakacheri server instantly during form entry.
  • Validity Alert: Caste certificates are valid for 5 years; Income certificates must be issued within 12 months.

SNQ Fee Waiver Declaration

Supernumerary Quota 100% Tuition Exemption

  • 5% extra seats reserved over and above sanctioned intake in all Karnataka engineering colleges.
  • Income Ceiling: Total annual family income from all sources must be strictly less than ₹8.00 Lakhs.
  • Income Certificate RD number must be actively validated in the application form to unlock SNQ seat options.

Step-by-Step SATS & RD Troubleshooting Protocol

If your SATS or RD number shows "Record Not Found" on the KEA portal: (1) Verify on nadakacheri.karnataka.gov.in that your digital certificate is approved and not pending at the Revenue Inspector level; (2) Ensure there are no spaces or punctuation within the 15-digit alphanumeric RD string; (3) If your name on SATS differs from your 10th Marks card, select "Non-SATS / Other" to manually enter credentials as per your Class 10 marksheet.

Exam Centre Allotment Protocol

KEA allots exam venues based on first-choice preference subject to seating limits in each district. If the first preference is saturated, the second or third preference is automatically assigned. Horanadu and Gadinadu candidates taking the Kannada Language Test will be assigned centres exclusively in Bengaluru, Belagavi, or Mangaluru.

50:50 Merit Weightage Formula

For Engineering & Technology admissions, KEA computes composite rank merit by combining 50% weightage from the KCET entrance exam (PCM marks out of 180) and 50% weightage from Class 12th / 2nd PUC Board PCM marks (normalized out of 100%). Prepare both your board syllabus and entrance speed accordingly.

Special Reservations & Article 371(J) Guidelines

Eligibility norms, documentation protocol, and in-person verification rules for special category and regional quotas.

Article 371(J) Kalyana-Karnataka

Regional & Statewide Reservation

  • Applicable to residents of Bidar, Kalaburagi, Yadgir, Raichur, Koppal, Ballari, and Vijayanagara.
  • Quota: 8% statewide engineering seats + 70% regional quota inside region colleges.
  • Must enter valid Form A Certificate reference number issued by Assistant Commissioner.

15% Rural Study Quota

Gram Panchayat Jurisdiction Schools

  • Candidate must have completed 10 full academic years of study (1st to 10th std) in a recognized rural school.
  • The institution must not be situated in any city corporation or town municipal council area.
  • Certificate issued by the Headmaster must be counter-signed by the concerned BEO.

5% Kannada Medium Quota

Mother Tongue Instruction

  • Candidate must have completed 10 full academic years of schooling (1st to 10th std) in Kannada medium.
  • Both government and government-recognized private schools in Karnataka qualify.
  • Certificate requires counter-signature by the Block Education Officer (BEO).

Special Category Quotas

Physical Submission at KEA Bengaluru

  • Quotas include NCC, Sports (National/State), Defence, Ex-Servicemen, Scouts & Guides, and PwD.
  • Declare eligibility in the online form during registration.
  • Mandatory Submission: Original documents must be submitted in person at KEA Office, Malleswaram within 7 days of form closure.

Critical Mistakes to Avoid in KCET Registration

Common errors that cause application rejection, loss of reservation seats, or conversion to General Merit during KEA verification.

SATS Data vs SSLC Marksheet Mismatch

Spelling mismatches in initials, space placement, or parent names between the SATS database and SSLC records cause automated verification failure during rank generation.

Remedy: Update the SATS database via your PU College administrative login before submitting the final KEA registration.

Expired or Invalid RD Numbers

Caste and Income certificates issued by Nadakacheri possess fixed legal validity periods (typically 5 years for caste, 1 year for income). Expired certificates fail real-time authentication.

Remedy: Verify your certificate active status on the official Nadakacheri portal (nadakacheri.karnataka.gov.in) prior to registration.

Incomplete 7-Year Study Entry

Entering only 2nd PUC details or skipping primary school academic history invalidates Karnataka Domicile Clause A, leading to automatic conversion to non-subsidized quotas.

Remedy: Enter every academic year sequentially from 1st standard to 12th standard, accounting for all seven required Karnataka years.

Omitting the SNQ Income Declaration

Leaving the annual family income column blank or entering "Not Applicable" permanently revokes your eligibility for the 5% Supernumerary Quota 100% tuition waiver.

Remedy: Always input the authentic family income from all sources and enter the matching Income Certificate RD number.

Submitting Multiple Applications

Candidates who make an error often register anew with a different mobile number. KEA algorithms detect duplicate Aadhaar/SSLC records and reject both applications.

Remedy: Do not create duplicate accounts. Wait for the official KEA Application Correction Window to modify any errors.

Uploading Selfies or Blurry Photos

Cropped smartphone selfies, photos with shadows, patterned backgrounds, or skewed signatures lead to hall ticket cancellation at examination centres.

Remedy: Obtain a professionally photographed passport picture against a solid white backdrop adhering to the 30–50 KB standard.

Post-Registration Candidate Lifecycle

Key milestones after submitting your KCET 2027 application leading up to physical document verification and counselling.

Milestone 1

Confirmation Page Archival

Print at least 3 copies of your KEA Confirmation Slip and payment transaction receipt. Retain one copy for physical submission at your BEO document verification center.

Milestone 2

Admit Card Download (April 2027)

Log in with your application number and date of birth to download your Hall Ticket. Verify exam session timings, reporting bell, and designated examination venue in your preferred city.

Milestone 3

Entrance Examination & Key Challenge

Appear for the offline exam using blue/black ballpoint pens. Check the provisional answer keys within 3 days and submit objections with textbook citations on the KEA portal.

Milestone 4

Rank Card & Score Declaration

KEA computes ranks using 50% CET marks + 50% 2nd PUC Board normalized marks. Download your official scorecard displaying engineering, agri, or pharma ranks.

Milestone 5

BEO Document Verification

Visit your local Block Education Officer (BEO) office with original study certificates, marks cards, and RD certificates to receive your official Verification Slip.

Milestone 6

Secret Key & Option Entry

Activate your counselling candidate portal using the 16-character Secret Key found on your verification slip and commence filling unlimited college-branch preferences.
