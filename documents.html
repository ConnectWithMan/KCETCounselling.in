Stage 2

7-Year Karnataka Study Certificate & BEO Counter-Signature

The 7-year schooling certificate is the statutory foundation of KCET eligibility under KEA Clause A. Understanding who signs and where to submit is vital to avoid disqualification.

1

School Level Issuance

Obtain study certificates from the Head of the Institution (Principal/Headmaster) for every school attended in Karnataka from 1st to 12th Standard / 2nd PUC.

2

SATS ID Verification

Ensure each certificate clearly prints your 9-digit Student Achievement Tracking System (SATS) number. SATS data must correspond with the Department of School Education database.

3

Jurisdictional BEO Seal

Submit state board certificates to the taluk Block Education Officer (BEO). For CBSE and ICSE, submit to the jurisdictional Deputy Director of Public Instruction (DDPI).

4

Secret Key Release

Once certified, KEA generates your official Verification Slip. This document contains your confidential 16-character Secret Key required to log into the Option Entry portal.

Karnataka State Board (KSEAB) School Headmaster / Junior College Principal Block Education Officer (BEO) Online SATS / Offline BEO If SATS data reflects 7 continuous years, online auto-verification triggers automatically. If SATS has gaps, visit BEO with physical certificates.
CBSE (Affiliated Schools in KA) School Principal DDPI / Designated BEO Physical DDPI Verification CBSE school certificates are NOT auto-verified by SATS. Parents must physically present study certificates to the district DDPI office for seals.

The 7 Cumulative Years Rule (Non-Continuous Allowed)

The 7 years of schooling in Karnataka do NOT have to be continuous. For instance, studying 1st to 4th Std in Bengaluru (4 years), 5th to 7th Std in Delhi (3 years), and 8th to 10th Std in Bengaluru (3 years) equals 7 cumulative years in Karnataka. You qualify fully under Clause A!

Stage 3

Reservation & Quota Evidentiary Certificates

Claiming category seats, tuition fee waivers (SNQ), or horizontal quotas requires authentic certificates issued by Karnataka revenue authorities. Inspect the exact form codes and validity boundaries below.

Caste & Income Certificates

Issued through the Nadakacheri portal by the Tahsildar. Mandatory for Category 1, 2A, 2B, 3A, 3B, SC, and ST candidates.

  • SC / ST: Form D (Permanent validity)
  • Category-1: Form E (5 Years validity)
  • Cat 2A, 2B, 3A, 3B: Form F (5 Years validity)
15-Digit RD Mandatory

Supernumerary Quota (SNQ)

Provides massive tuition fee waivers (up to 95% discount). Open to all categories including General Merit.

  • Income Limit: Family income strictly below ₹8.00 Lakhs per annum
  • Document: Income Certificate with RD number
  • Validity: Valid on the date of option entry
5% Supernumerary Seats

Rural Reservation (15%)

Available for candidates who studied 10 full academic years (1st to 10th std) in schools situated in designated rural areas.

  • Prescribed Format: Form-2 (Annexure)
  • School Location: Outside CMC, TMC & Municipal limits
  • Endorsement: BEO Counter-Signature mandatory
15% State Quota

Kannada Medium Quota (5%)

10 full academic years (1st to 10th standard) completed with Kannada as the sole medium of instruction.

  • Format: Kannada Medium Certificate signed by Headmaster
  • Counter-Seal: Jurisdictional BEO seal required
  • Exemption: Exempt from Kannada language entrance test
5% State Quota

Kalyana Karnataka (Article 371J)

Local district reservation in Bidar, Kalaburagi, Yadgir, Raichur, Koppal, Ballari, and Vijayanagara.

  • Prescribed Certificate: Annexure-A Eligibility Certificate
  • Issuing Authority: Assistant Commissioner of Revenue
  • Reservation: 70% in local region, 8% statewide
Article 371J

EWS (Economically Weaker Section)

10% quota for General Category candidates not covered under SC, ST, or OBC reservation frameworks.

  • Gross Income: Below ₹8.00 Lakhs per annum
  • Asset Limits: Less than 5 acres agricultural land / 1000 sq ft flat
  • Issuing Authority: Jurisdictional Tahsildar with RD No.
10% Central/State Seats
Stage 4

KEA Statutory Domicile Clauses (A to O) Document Directory

Every candidate claiming government quota seats must qualify under at least one statutory clause. Find your applicable clause and the mandatory supporting evidentiary papers.

CLAUSE A Primary Route (90% Applicants)

Karnataka 7-Year Regular Study Rule

Candidate studied 7 full academic years in Karnataka and passed 10th (SSLC) or 12th (PUC) from Karnataka.

Required Documents

1) 7-Year Study Certificate (1st to 12th Std) counter-signed by BEO/DDPI with SATS ID.
2) SSLC / 10th Marks Card.
3) 2nd PUC / 12th Marks Card.

CLAUSE B Parents Studied 7 Years

Candidate 2 Years + Parent 7 Years in Karnataka

Candidate passed 11th & 12th (or 1st & 2nd PUC) in Karnataka, and either parent studied 7 years in Karnataka.

Required Documents

1) Candidate's 2-Year Karnataka Study Certificate (PUC).
2) Parent's 7-Year Karnataka Study Certificate / Domicile Certificate from Tahsildar.
3) Parent's SSLC marks card or Transfer Certificate (proof of identity & study).

CLAUSE C Horanadu Kannadiga

Mother Tongue Kannada / Tulu / Kodava Outside Karnataka

Candidate residing outside Karnataka with mother tongue Kannada/Tulu/Kodava whose parent resided in Karnataka for 10 years.

Required Documents

1) Mother tongue affidavit on ₹100 stamp paper.
2) Parent's 10-year Karnataka Domicile Certificate.
3) Candidate's 10+2 marks card from outside Karnataka.
4) KEA Kannada Language Test Score Card (Min 12/50 marks).

CLAUSE D Gadinadu Kannadiga

Disputed Border Area Kannada Residents

Candidate residing in designated border taluks (e.g., Kasaragod, Maharashtra borders) with mother tongue Kannada/Tulu/Kodava.

Required Documents

1) Border residence certificate from local Revenue Authority.
2) Mother tongue affidavit on stamp paper.
3) Qualifying 10+2 examination certificate.
4) KEA Kannada Language Test Score Card (Min 12/50 marks).

CLAUSE E & F Defence Personnel

Serving Defence & Ex-Servicemen Posted in Karnataka

Children of defence personnel serving in Karnataka or ex-servicemen declared Karnataka as their home hometown.

Required Documents

1) Certificate from Commanding Officer (CO) certifying posting in Karnataka.
2) Ex-Servicemen: Discharge Certificate, PPO & Sainik Welfare Board Certificate.
3) Proof of candidate passing 10+2 in Karnataka (Clause E).

CLAUSE G TO O Central Govt / CAPF / Clause O

Central Govt, CAPF, MPs & Kannada Medium Outside KA

Children of Union Government employees, All India Services, CAPF stationed in Karnataka, or 10 years Kannada study outside KA.

Required Documents

1) Employment Certificate & Transfer Orders showing 1-year continuous service in KA.
2) Candidate's 2-year study in Karnataka.
3) Clause O: 10-year Kannada medium study certificate certified by respective state education director.

Stage 6

Final College Reporting: Mandatory Original Documents

Upon freezing a seat under Choice 1, candidates must report to their allotted engineering college before the deadline with all original transcripts. The college retains originals until university registration.

1. KEA Allotment & Receipts

  • KEA Admission Order: Choice 1 confirmation printout with college seal.
  • KEA Document Verification Slip: Showing 16-character Secret Key.
  • Tuition Fee Challan / Receipt: Original bank counterfoil or payment confirmation.

2. Academic Transcripts

  • 10th / SSLC Marks Card: Original date of birth and parent identity record.
  • 12th / 2nd PUC Marks Card: Original qualifying science score card.
  • 7-Year Study Certificate: Original with BEO / DDPI official seal.

3. Institutional Transfer Proofs

  • Transfer Certificate (TC): Original from the last attended PU / Junior College.
  • Migration Certificate: Mandatory for CBSE, ICSE, and other non-state boards.
  • Conduct / Character Certificate: Issued by the head of the previous institution.

4. Medical & Legal Undertakings

  • Medical Fitness Certificate: Signed by registered MBBS medical doctor.
  • Anti-Ragging Affidavits: Signed undertakings by candidate & parent (online UGC ref).
  • Photographs & Identity: Aadhaar Card original + 6 recent passport size photos.
Critical Guidance

Top 10 Document Verification Pitfalls & Rejection Triggers

Every year, hundreds of deserving candidates lose their engineering rank or category reservation due to minor clerical discrepancies. Review these proven danger zones to protect your candidacy.

1. Name Mismatch with Aadhaar / 10th Marks Card

Initial expansion vs. no initials between Aadhaar and SSLC marks card causes KEA software to halt validation.

Solution: Update Aadhaar before form submission or get a ₹100 Notary Affidavit.

2. Missing DDPI Counter-Seal on CBSE/ICSE Certificates

Presenting study certificates signed ONLY by the CBSE/ICSE school Principal leads to instant rejection.

Solution: DDPI or designated BEO counter-signature is statutorily mandatory!

3. Expired Income Certificate for Category / SNQ

Income and Category-cum-Income certificates (Form E, Form F) have a strict 5-year validity. Older RD numbers fail API queries.

Solution: Renew your Nadakacheri income certificate if issued prior to 2022.

4. Rural School Upgraded to Municipal Town

If the school area was upgraded to a City Municipal Council (CMC) during your 1st–10th study period, rural reservation is void.

Solution: Check the school's historical village panchayat notification status with the BEO.

5. Missing Migration Certificate for Non-State Boards

Colleges cannot complete university enrollment under VTU or autonomous bodies without the original Migration Certificate.

Solution: Download official digital migration from DigiLocker or request your board.

6. Missing SATS Number on School Certificates

Study certificates without the 9-digit Student Achievement Tracking System (SATS) code cannot be verified in the KEA portal.

Solution: Request your school administration to generate and print your official SATS number.

An RD Number (Revenue Department Number) is a 15-character alphanumeric GSC code (e.g., RD0038...) generated on certificates issued through the Karnataka Government Nadakacheri (Atalji Janasnehi Kendra) portal. KEA software connects directly to the Revenue Department API during online registration; entering a valid RD number automatically fetches and verifies your Caste, Income, SNQ, and Hyderabad-Karnataka (371J) eligibility in real time without needing physical uploads.

In Karnataka, SC and ST caste certificates (Form D) have lifetime validity. If your certificate was issued prior to 2022, you must obtain a renewed RD certificate from Nadakacheri before KCET registration.

Candidates must have studied from 1st standard to 10th standard in Kannada as the medium of instruction. The candidate must obtain the Kannada Medium Study Certificate signed by the Headmaster/Principal of the schools attended and counter-signed by the jurisdictional Block Education Officer (BEO).

To claim local reservation under Article 371J for the Hyderabad-Karnataka region (Bidar, Kalaburagi, Yadgir, Raichur, Koppal, Ballari, and Vijayanagara), candidates must produce the Annexure-A Eligibility Certificate. This certificate must be issued by the Assistant Commissioner of the Revenue Sub-Division with an authentic RD number.

Special category documents are NOT verified online or at local BEO offices. SATS (Student Achievement Tracking System) auto-verification is available for students whose schools have entered complete, error-free 7-year academic history into the Department of School Education SATS portal. Discrepancies or CBSE/ICSE students require manual BEO verification.

During final admission under Choice 1, colleges require: 1) KEA Final Allotment Letter / Admission Order, 2) KEA Document Verification Slip with Secret Key, 3) Original Fee Paid Bank Challan/Receipt, 4) Original SSLC Marks Card, 5) Original 2nd PUC / 12th Marks Card, 6) Original 7-Year Study Certificate (BEO signed), 7) Original Transfer Certificate (TC), 8) Migration Certificate (for CBSE/ICSE/Non-State boards), 9) Medical Fitness Certificate, 10) Anti-Ragging Undertakings, and 3 sets of attested photocopies.
